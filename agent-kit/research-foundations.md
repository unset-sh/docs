The review combines psychological frameworks, expert heuristics and accessibility guidance. The seven perspectives and reporting method are practical design choices; they are not a validated psychological scale.

| Source | Contribution | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Ryan and Deci, self-determination theory](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2000_RyanDeci_SDT.pdf) | Autonomy, competence and relatedness motivate questions about choice, effectiveness and connection. | A particular feature does not guarantee need satisfaction. |
| [Nielsen, usability heuristics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/) | Visibility, familiar language, consistency, control and recovery suggest observable checks. | Heuristics are expert rules of thumb, not universal causal laws. |
| [Hassenzahl and colleagues, 2010](https://academic.oup.com/iwc/article-abstract/22/5/353/684432?login=false) | Need fulfilment was associated with positive feelings in recalled technology experiences. | The correlational findings do not prove that a feature causes delight. |
| [Norman, emotional design](https://jnd.org/emotional-design-people-and-things/) | Appearance, interaction and reflective meaning provide separate questions for a journey. | The framework does not calculate emotional quality. |
| [W3C, WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) | Accessibility criteria inform checks under specified input and display conditions. | A partial agent review is not a conformance audit or a simulation of disability. |
| [Peters, Calvo and Ryan, METUX](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.00797/full) | Separates immediate interaction from broader effects on people's lives. | Engagement alone is insufficient evidence of well-being. |

Visual appeal and usability should be assessed separately. [Tuch and colleagues](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chb.2012.03.024) found context-dependent effects between perceived beauty and usability. An attractive screenshot does not establish how a task feels to complete.

**Why retain human testing**

[SimUser](https://doi.org/10.1145/3613904.3642481) demonstrated potential for generating usability feedback within a limited smartwatch study. [Research on synthetic HCI responses](https://doi.org/10.1145/3544548.3580688) supports their use for ideation and piloting while requiring validation with real data.

[Research on conversational user simulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11245) found systematic differences from human behaviour and that greater general model capability did not guarantee more faithful simulation. Those results concern conversational tasks; they do not establish performance on visual app reviews.

For a subsequent human study, the [User Experience Questionnaire](https://ueq-online.org/) offers a standard instrument and handbook. Generated agent answers must not be treated as participant responses. Use [the interpretation guide](interpreting-an-experience-review.md) to turn review findings into neutral research questions.
